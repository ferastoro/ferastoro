# Ferastoro | Data Science & AI Engineering

> Teaching machines to learn while I figure out how I learn.

***

Hi, I'm Fera (Ferrari Meilani), an Information Systems student at Universitas Hasanuddin in Makassar, Indonesia. I like machine learning, deep learning, and building things with data. I'm still figuring out how much of it I actually understand and how much just happens to converge.

Outside of code, I get curious about philosophy and why people behave the way they do. Turns out that helps when you build systems meant to work with people, and it also gives me something to overthink at 2 AM.

***

### What I'm Into

- **AI Engineering & Deep Learning**: building and tuning neural models, and learning why they work (or don't).
- **Data Science**: messy datasets, time-series analysis, and dashboards that people can actually read.
- **Philosophy & Human Behavior**: how people and intelligent systems think, decide, and sometimes get it wrong.
